# Roadmap

## Current foundation

**Being qualified:**
- repository identity and legal texts;
- architecture and governance;
- native documentation;
- dedicated validation tools.

**Domain capabilities and claims:** empty.

## Capability milestones

1. **The nine planned v1 record schemas with golden fixtures,** starting with `circuit_automation.autonomy_envelope.v1` for prototyping-board and bench input-surface clamping.
2. **Surface-clamp validator and safe local refusal-probe harness** for enumerated JTAG, overlay and debug-server surfaces.
3. **Separate-process `SECS/GEM` and `OPC UA` simulator/adapter harnesses** plus `LOTO` and sub-`50 V` recovery validators.

**Enrolled with every implementation change:**
- strict native typing and linting;
- native documentation;
- meaningful statement and branch coverage;
- affected boundary tests and benchmarks.

**Requires its own evidence and explicit approval:**
- enabling any physical board, bench instrument or workcell;
- post-fault cell reconciliation and `LOTO` clearance;
- public activation and release.

No availability date is promised. No performance, yield or productivity gain is promised either.
